## Neutrophil maturation examples

Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019). Deep learning approach to peripheral leukocyte recognition. PLoS ONE 14(6): e0218808.

https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0218808

One complete rectangular cell-image panel cropped from Figure 2 and WebP-compressed (178 × 178 pixels). License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). No recoloring or AI enhancement. These are separate examples, not time-lapse photographs of a single maturing cell.

- neutrophil.webp: Figure 1, PBC row, neutrophil column; Cheong MYY et al. (2026); PBC dataset: Acevedo et al.
- lymphocyte.webp: Figure 1, PBC row, lymphocyte column; Cheong MYY et al. (2026); PBC dataset: Acevedo et al.
- monocyte.webp: Figure 1, PBC row, monocyte column; Cheong MYY et al. (2026); PBC dataset: Acevedo et al.
- eosinophil.webp: Figure 1, PBC row, eosinophil column; Cheong MYY et al. (2026); PBC dataset: Acevedo et al.
- basophil.webp: Figure 1, PBC row, basophil column; Cheong MYY et al. (2026); PBC dataset: Acevedo et al.
- promyelocyte.webp: B, row 1; Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019)
- myelocyte.webp: C, row 1; Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019)
- metamyelocyte.webp: D, row 1; Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019)
- band.webp: E, row 1; Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019)
- neutrophil-stage.webp: F, row 1; Wang Q, Bi S, Sun M, Wang Y, Wang D, Yang S (2019)

Published microscopy represents selected cells, not proof of a healthy donor. Images are not at a common on-screen scale. The generic blast photograph in Wang et al. panel A is deliberately not used as a normal myeloblast.

All diagrams are original simplified teaching illustrations. B, T, and NK identities are not inferred from microscopy.
