# Red cell morphology: photographs and teaching references

All images are real human blood-film microscopy. The five new JPEGs are resized to a maximum dimension of 1400 pixels and JPEG-compressed for web delivery; no crop, recoloring, AI generation, or enhancement. Full fields are retained. Original-resolution downloads remain available through the source links below. The normal image reuses the approved full-field WebP. Original embedded annotations are retained, but no calibration or cross-photo size comparison is claimed.
