# Virus teaching visuals

All SVG drawings and animation artwork were created for Inside the Laboratory. They are explanatory schematics, not microscopy, exact molecular reconstructions, or diagnostic images. Colors are arbitrary.

The infection cycle models entry through an endosome, fusion/uncoating, cytoplasmic positive-sense RNA replication, assembly, and plasma-membrane budding, as in an alphavirus. It intentionally omits molecular detail and is not a universal RNA-virus cycle.

## October 4 revision: lysis and mRNA vaccines
The infection animation now uses a simplified non-enveloped, picornavirus-style lytic pathway, replacing the previous alphavirus budding model. Primary teaching references: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK7687/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21762819/ . Counts and timing are illustrative.
Original vaccine SVG diagrams explain conventional mRNA COVID-19 vaccines. Reviewed sources and limitations are linked on rna-vaccines.html, including CDC vaccine basics, safety monitoring, clinical safety considerations, and CHOP Vaccine Education Center.
