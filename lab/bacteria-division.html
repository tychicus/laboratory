The power of doubling

Adding? No. Multiplying.

Each generation doubles the population: 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 16. Some E. coli can divide about every 20 minutes in favorable laboratory conditions. Other bacteria take much longer.

Why the real world puts on the brakes.

This model starts with one cell, doubles every 20 minutes, and assumes unlimited nutrients and space, with no deaths or delay. Real cultures eventually face depleted nutrients and accumulating waste. Growth slows; cells may die. This is not a prediction of an infection inside a person.
